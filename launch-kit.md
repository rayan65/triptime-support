## Product facts

Free iPhone app for elapsed trip timing with a CarPlay widget, pause/resume, stop breakdowns, reconnect grace periods, trip history and weekly summaries. Automatic timing requires one-time creation of two personal automations in Shortcuts. CarPlay widgets require iOS 26 or later and a compatible CarPlay display. No account or location tracking. It does not currently measure distance, speed, fuel or routes. In-car time is trip time minus recorded stops and pauses; it is not proof of vehicle movement.

## Community post

Title: I made a free CarPlay trip timer with a grace period for short stops

I’m the developer of Trip Insights, previously called TripTime by Rayan.

I wanted to see how long a journey had taken without checking my phone. The app puts elapsed time on a CarPlay widget and saves trip history with start/end times and stop breakdowns.

A configurable grace period keeps a short CarPlay disconnection from splitting one journey into two. There is also manual pause/resume.

Setup matters: you create two personal automations in Shortcuts once—CarPlay Connects → Start Trip, and CarPlay Disconnects → CarPlay Disconnected, both Run Immediately. CarPlay widgets require iOS 26 or later and a compatible display.

The app is free, with no account or location tracking. It does not measure speed or mileage.

I’d particularly appreciate feedback on readability on different car displays and whether the setup instructions are clear.

## Publishing checklist

- Verify the App Store listing is accessible to the intended audience and check the current displayed name.
- Use current genuine screenshots; no invented UI or claims about planned features.
- Disclose that this is your app in community posts.
- Check each community’s current promotion rules and account eligibility before posting.
- No paid promotion, review incentives, bought votes or unsolicited bulk messages.
- Product Hunt draft is prepared, not submitted. A verified personal account is needed.
- Social copy is prepared, not posted.
- Compare App Store downloads before and after each channel; do not claim impressions or installs without data.
