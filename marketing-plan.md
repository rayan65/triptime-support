# Trip Insights: zero-budget marketing plan

## Goal and positioning
Help CarPlay users discover, install and successfully set up Trip Insights.
Positioning: a free trip timer on your car display, with stops and trip history.
Audience priority: CarPlay commuters; drivers who make short stops; privacy-conscious iPhone users. English and Saudi Arabic messaging.
Budget: zero. No ads, paid placements or purchases.

## Product truth
Automatic timing requires two personal automations in Shortcuts.
CarPlay widgets require iOS 26+ and a compatible CarPlay display.
No current speed, mileage, fuel or location features. Do not market planned features as released.
Use genuine current UI for product visuals. Never invent downloads, testimonials, ratings or endorsements.
Verify current App Store name and availability before announcements.

## Execution priorities
1. Owned website: clear benefit, download link, searchable setup help, Arabic introduction and working support.
2. Setup education: publish a practical tutorial and answer common reconnect/automation questions.
3. Existing social accounts: publish a launch post and a genuine parked-car demonstration when account access and real media are available.
4. Product Hunt: use launch-kit.md; verify account and required assets before submitting. Free submission is documented in its official help.
5. Relevant communities: research current posting rules and account eligibility. Publish only where developer promotion is permitted, disclosing ownership. No mass cross-posting or unsolicited messages.
6. App Store optimization: inspect public listing and recommend accurate improvements. Do not withdraw reviews, change releases or trigger native builds for marketing.
7. Iterate based on measured downloads, setup questions and genuine user feedback.

## First 14 days
Days 1–3: verify website deployment; publish setup tutorial; establish baseline if App Store Connect metrics are available; prepare account-specific posts.
Days 4–7: publish to accessible approved accounts; prepare/submit Product Hunt when ready; research up to three well-matched communities, verifying rules individually.
Days 8–10: produce one useful setup tip or real demonstration; address repeated questions; improve confusing website copy.
Days 11–14: compare results by available evidence; continue useful channels and drop unproductive work. Do not manufacture activity to fill a report.

## Daily operating loop
Read current files and recent work before changing anything. Verify live product facts.
Choose one highest-impact executable task, complete it and verify its result.
If external publishing is blocked by login, complete independent work and report the exact blocker once.
Track published URLs and distinguish drafts from published work.
Check accessible public reviews and mentions. Use App Store Connect metrics only with authorized access.
Never expose private contact details, credentials or native source in this public repository.

## Scorecard
Primary: first-time App Store downloads and successful setup feedback, where measurable.
Secondary: verified published placements, genuine questions/reviews, referral or campaign results when available.
Baseline currently unknown: no authenticated App Store Connect analytics access in this session.
No website visitor analytics installed. Deployment success is not traffic or acquisition.
A draft or committed page is not an external social placement.

## Report format
