Trip Insights Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

The short version

Trip Insights collects nothing. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no networking code. Nothing it records leaves your iPhone.

What Trip Insights stores

  • The start and end time of each trip, and its duration.
  • Each pause: when it began, when it ended, and whether it was a pause you made or a CarPlay disconnection.
  • Your settings: timer text size and the reconnect grace period.

That is the whole of it. This data is written to a single file in Trip Insights's own app group container on the device, so the app and its widget can both read it. It is protected by iOS data protection and is available only after you have unlocked your iPhone at least once since it was restarted.

What Trip Insights does not do

  • No location. The app contains no location features and links no location framework. It cannot see where you are or where you have been.
  • No network. There is no networking code in the app. It cannot send anything anywhere, with or without your knowledge.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no identifiers.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.
  • No permissions. Trip Insights asks for nothing.

Sharing

None. There is nobody to share with: no servers, no processors, no partners.

Your control over the data

Delete any individual trip, or clear your whole history, from inside the app. Deleting Trip Insights removes everything it stored, including its app group container.

Children

Trip Insights is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

Support

For help or privacy questions, open a support issue on GitHub. Issues are public; please do not include personal information.
