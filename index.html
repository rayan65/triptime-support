TripTime Support

An elapsed-time tracker for car journeys. No location, no account, no network.

Timing a trip

Tap Start trip in the app, or on the widget. The timer counts total journey time, including stops. Tap End trip to finish; the trip is saved to your history on this iPhone.

Pausing

Pause keeps the trip and its original start time, freezes the timer and shows how long you have been paused. There is no deadline — the trip stays paused until you resume or end it, and reconnecting CarPlay will not override it. Resume counts the gap as stop time and records it in the trip's stop breakdown.

Automatic timing with CarPlay

iOS does not let an app start itself when your car connects, so you create two personal automations once, in the Shortcuts app:

TriggerRun modeAction
CarPlay → ConnectsRun ImmediatelyTripTime → Start Trip
CarPlay → DisconnectsRun ImmediatelyTripTime → CarPlay Disconnected

Use CarPlay Disconnected, not End Trip. End Trip finishes a trip immediately and cannot be resumed, so a brief dropout would split one drive in two.

The reconnect grace period

After a CarPlay disconnect the trip waits for the period you choose — off, 5 minutes, 10 minutes, or your own. Reconnect inside that window and the same trip resumes, counting the gap as stop time. If the window passes, the trip is recorded as ending at the moment the car disconnected, and the waiting time is not counted.

The widget

Add TripTime to your Home Screen, and on iOS 26 or later to the CarPlay widget area via Settings → General → CarPlay → your car → Widgets. Choose Small, Medium or Large timer text in the app's settings.

Common questions

The timer did not start when I got in the car. Check the two automations exist and are set to Run Immediately. After restarting your iPhone, unlock it once before the automations will run.

A trip is still running. If a disconnect was missed, end it in the app.

Does it track where I go? No. TripTime has no location features of any kind and requests no permissions.
