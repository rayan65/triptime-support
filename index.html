TripTime Support

An elapsed-time tracker for car journeys. No location, no account, no network.

What the home screen shows

TripTime opens on its CarPlay card, because timing itself is what the app is for. Once you have created the two automations below, tap I’ve created the automations and the card folds down to a compact setup-and-help row. That is only your note to the app: TripTime cannot see your automations and never claims to have checked them. You can bring the full card back from Settings.

While a trip is running it takes the top of the screen, with the timer, the start time, in-car time and stops.

Timing a trip

Trips normally start on their own. To start one by hand, tap Start manually in the app, or Start Trip on the widget. The timer counts total journey time, including stops. Tap Finish Trip Now to finish — the widget's button is the shorter Finish Now — and the trip is saved to your history on this iPhone.

In-car time is that journey time with your stops and pauses taken out. It is arithmetic on the times recorded, not detected driving: TripTime cannot tell whether the car was moving.

Pausing

Pause keeps the trip and its original start time, freezes the timer and shows how long you have been paused. There is no deadline — the trip stays paused until you resume or end it, and reconnecting CarPlay will not override it. Resume counts the gap as stop time and records it in the trip's stop breakdown.

Automatic timing with CarPlay

iOS does not let an app start itself when your car connects, so you create two personal automations once, in the Shortcuts app:

TriggerRun modeAction
CarPlay → ConnectsRun ImmediatelyTripTime → Start Trip
CarPlay → DisconnectsRun ImmediatelyTripTime → CarPlay Disconnected

Use CarPlay Disconnected, not Finish Trip Now. Finish Trip Now ends a trip immediately and it cannot be resumed, so a brief dropout would split one drive in two. The app's Set Up CarPlay screen, under the Settings button, opens Shortcuts and lists these steps. iOS requires you to create the automations yourself; no app is allowed to add them for you.

The reconnect grace period

After a CarPlay disconnect the trip waits for the period you choose — off, 5 minutes, 10 minutes, or your own. Reconnect inside that window and the same trip resumes, counting the gap as stop time. If the window passes, the trip is recorded as ending at the moment the car disconnected, and the waiting time is not counted.

The widget

Add TripTime to your Home Screen, and on iOS 26 or later to the CarPlay widget area via Settings → General → CarPlay → your car → Widgets. Choose Small, Medium or Large timer text under the Settings button in the app's title bar, which also holds the reconnect grace period and CarPlay setup.

Deleting trips

Tap the trash icon on a trip and that row turns into its own confirmation, keeping the trip’s date, times and duration on screen so there is no doubt which one is going. Only one row can be asking at a time. Clear at the top of Trip history removes everything and asks separately.

Common questions

The timer did not start when I got in the car. Check the two automations exist and are set to Run Immediately. After restarting your iPhone, unlock it once before the automations will run.

A trip is still running. If a disconnect was missed, end it in the app.

Does it track where I go? No. TripTime has no location features of any kind and requests no permissions.
